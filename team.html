Robert Potts

Managing Partner

Robert Potts is the Managing Partner at Accelerated Insurance & Risk Management Services (AIRMS). He brings deep expertise in the transportation insurance sector, having spent the majority of his career at Dalton Timmis Insurance Group — including roles as Transportation Specialist and Vice President of Transportation Sales — before serving as Vice President of Transportation at NFP Canada.

Robert leads the brokerage with a focus on long-term client partnerships, ensuring every business receives expert advice and coverage that matches the real risks of their operations. His carrier relationships and hands-on knowledge of transportation and commercial lines set the foundation for everything AIRMS delivers.

Kassa Kebede is Vice President of Commercial Insurance at AIRMS, where he leads the commercial lines practice with a focus on delivering tailored risk management solutions for businesses across Canada.

Kassa works across a wide range of commercial insurance lines including general liability, property, professional liability, cyber, and more. His consultative approach ensures clients secure the right coverage for their specific operations and risk profile.

Kassa holds his RIBO (Registered Insurance Brokers of Ontario) license and works closely with leading Canadian and international insurers to negotiate competitive terms on behalf of his clients.

Andrew J. Soares

Vice President, Transportation & Commercial Lines

Andrew Soares is Vice President of Transportation & Commercial Lines at AIRMS. He holds a Bachelor of Commerce from Ted Rogers School of Management (Toronto Metropolitan University) and is a licensed broker with RIBO and the Alberta Insurance Council.

Andrew's focus is helping trucking and transportation companies protect their operations while navigating an increasingly complex insurance market. He works closely with fleet owners and operators to deliver strategic insurance solutions tailored to their risk profile, growth plans, and long-term goals.

His approach is relationship-driven and grounded in a deep understanding of transportation operations — from owner-operators to growing fleets. Andrew is also an active member of the Fleet Safety Council (Toronto Chapter).

Manny Soares

Vice President & Partner

Manny Soares is a Vice President and Partner at AIRMS, holding his RIBO designation with a career dedicated to transportation insurance. Prior to joining AIRMS, Manny held senior leadership roles at NFP (Vice President, Commercial Lines – Transportation) and Hallmark/McLean Hallmark Insurance Group (Vice President & Partner), where he built a strong reputation as one of Canada's leading transportation insurance specialists.

Manny's expertise spans fleet risk management, complex program structuring, and long-term client partnerships. His ability to understand the full picture of a client's operations ensures comprehensive, cost-effective coverage that genuinely protects their business.
